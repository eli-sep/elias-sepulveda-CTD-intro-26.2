About

Hi, I'm Elias.

I'm a Control Systems Engineer and aspiring software developer who believes technology should be used to serve people and make a positive difference in the world. My background is in industrial automation and building controls, where I've spent years solving complex technical problems and helping organizations operate more efficiently.

Today, I'm expanding my skills into web development through hands-on projects and continuous learning. I'm currently studying and building applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, APIs, and databases, and I enjoy the challenge of turning ideas into working software.

My desire to pursue technology is closely connected to my faith and personal values. I believe that every person has been given gifts and talents to serve others, and I view my career as an opportunity to do exactly that. Whatever path my professional journey takes, my goal is not simply to build successful software, but to create solutions that are meaningful, helpful, and contribute to the well-being of individuals and communities.

I am committed to lifelong learning, personal growth, and using technology in ways that reflect service, compassion, and purpose.
